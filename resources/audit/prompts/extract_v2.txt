Establish the listed facts from the dossier files. Each file is listed as F<n>: <name>, and each of its pages starts with a marker [F<n> p.<k>]. For every fact you find, return its key, its value exactly as written in the source, the file id (F<n>), the page number from the marker, and a verbatim quote of 10-400 characters copied from that page that contains the value. A fact whose line ends with "— număr (<unit>)" takes the number alone as its value, stated in that unit in the quote; one whose line ends with "— an" takes a four-digit year. A fact whose line ends with "— pasaj" is a description: its quote is the whole passage that describes it, every sentence copied verbatim from one page, up to 1500 characters, and its value is an empty string. Return one such fact per distinct passage, up to 6 for one key and up to 12 for audit.process_sections, in dossier order: one per stage of the technological flow, one per utility description, and a longer description split at a paragraph break. Never infer, compute, convert or translate a value that is not written in a source. List under missing every key you cannot find. When the task lists rejected facts with a reason and the text of the cited page, return a corrected fact or list its key under missing.

Where each section's facts usually are:
- ch2.date_generale: the Necesar info workbook, the registration documents and the environmental permit; the work regime (shifts, working days and hours) in the Necesar info, the permit or the facility data sheet (Fişa de date).
- ch2.manager: the energy manager's appointment or contract, else the Necesar info.
- ch2.activitate, ch2.istorie: the company presentation, the Fişa de date and the permit's description of the activity.
- ch2.localizare: the permit's site description (amplasament, vecinătăţi) and the urbanism or land documents.
- ch3.flux, ch3.process: the flow documents (the technological flow schemes and the Fişa de date's Flux sections) and the permit's description of the installations. Take a passage for each stage of each flow and for each production section the permit describes, a secondary one (a solvent, paint or recovery section) included.
- ch3.utilitati, ch3.apa, ch3.electricitate, ch3.gaz, ch3.carburant: the permit's utilities chapter, the Fişa de date and the supply contracts. A photovoltaic plant's installed power and commissioning year are in the permit, its connection documents or the Necesar info. The boiler rooms (centrale termice: their boilers, type, power and fuel) are in the permit or the Necesar info.
- ch3.aer_comprimat, ch3.climatizare, ch3.iluminat: the permit's description of the installations and the Necesar info.
- ch3.parc_auto, ch3.automatizare: the Necesar info and the permit.
- ch3.contorizare: the permit's monitoring section and the Necesar info: each meter, the resource it measures and where it is.
- ch3.consumatori, ch3.equipment: the Necesar info's equipment sheets and the permit's equipment lists: the main energy consumers with their power.
